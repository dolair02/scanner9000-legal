What SCAN-PRO 9000 is

SCAN-PRO 9000 is a police-scanner style radio simulator for entertainment and learning. It is a simulator: the radio calls are sample audio that comes with the app, and the channels, the dispatch log, the 911-style board, the aircraft and the cameras are simulated or use stock pictures and video. The app does not receive live police, fire or EMS radio. The weather alerts come from the U.S. National Weather Service, the radar pictures come from NOAA through the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, the map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, and the news streams are public YouTube live streams.
