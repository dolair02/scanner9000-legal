Common questions

Is this real police, fire or EMS radio?
No. SCAN-PRO 9000 is a simulator. The radio calls are sample audio that comes with the app, and the channels, dispatch log, 911-style board, aircraft and cameras are simulated. The weather alerts, the radar and the news streams are real. Never use the app in an emergency; call 911 or your local emergency number.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose SCAN-PRO 9000 and tap Cancel subscription. If you cancel during the free trial before it ends, you are not charged. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
I subscribed but the app asks me to subscribe again.
Open the subscription screen and tap Restore purchases. Make sure Google Play on your phone is signed in to the same Google account that bought the subscription.
I have an access code.
On the subscription screen tap “Have an access code?” and type it in.
The weather alerts or the radar do not load.
They come from outside services (the National Weather Service and NOAA radar through the Iowa Environmental Mesonet), so your phone needs an internet connection and the services need to be available. Try again in a few minutes.
A news stream does not play.
The streams are public YouTube live streams, and a channel can end or change its stream at any time. Use the “watch on YouTube” link under the stream to open it in your browser or the YouTube app.
How do I delete my data?
The app stores its data only on your phone, so uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it. We hold no data about you on any server.
