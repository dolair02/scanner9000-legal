SCAN-PRO 9000

Radio scanner simulator for Android · by Dolair

SCAN-PRO 9000 is a police-scanner style radio simulator for emergency-radio fans, for Android. Tune through simulated police, fire, EMS and weather channels with sample audio, follow a dispatch log with 10-codes, and watch a 911-style board, flight radar and cameras. The weather alerts and radar are real.

Real and simulated

  • Real: weather alerts from the U.S. National Weather Service, NOAA weather radar, OpenStreetMap maps and public YouTube news streams.
  • Simulated: radio calls (sample audio that comes with the app), the dispatch log, the 911-style board, aircraft and cameras.

For entertainment and learning only. Never use SCAN-PRO 9000 in an emergency or for a safety decision.

Subscription

SCAN-PRO Elite is sold through Google Play as a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. You can cancel any time in Google Play.
